# Invite Team Members

Collaborating on ECTickets is built around **Organizer Teams**. As a team admin, you can invite colleagues, staff, or co-organizers to help manage events, purchase tokens, and run day-of operations — all from a shared Team Management Dashboard.

This guide walks through sending an invitation from **Team settings**, explains the available roles, and covers what your invitee should expect. If you were invited to join an existing team, follow the steps in [Accepting a Team Invitation](#accepting-a-team-invitation) below.

> [!TIP]
>
> Need help? Our [Support team](https://www.ectickets.com/contact) is available.

## Before You Start

Make sure the following are in place:

- You have an **Admin** role on the organizer team (only admins can invite or remove members).
- Your team is set up — see [Account Setup](/docs/organizer/getting-started/account-setup) if you're new.
- You know the invitee's email address. They must use this same address when signing up or logging in.

<Callout type="info">
  Event tokens and subscription quota are shared at the team level.
  Invited members can help create events and manage sales, but billing
  actions depend on their role. See [Pricing](/docs/organizer/pricing)
  for plan details.
</Callout>

## Navigate to Team Settings

After logging in to the [Organizer Dashboard](https://www.ectickets.com/dashboard), open the **Team settings** page from the side menu. This is the same area where you manage subscription quota and purchase event tokens — see [Dashboard Navigation](/docs/organizer/getting-started/dashboard-navigation) for an overview of the Team Management Dashboard.

![Team settings page](/assets/docs/organizer/invite-member/team-settings-page.png)<br></br>
_Screenshot: Team settings with subscription, current members, and the invite form._

The page has three sections:

1. **Subscription** — current plan and available event quota.
2. **Team members** — everyone currently on the team, with their role and a **Remove** action.
3. **Invite team member** — the form used to send a new invitation.

## Send an Invitation

<Steps>
### Open the invite form

In **Team settings**, scroll to the **Invite team member** section at the bottom of the page.

### Enter the email address

Type the invitee's email in the **Email address** field (e.g., `user@ectickets.com`). Use the address they will sign up or log in with — mismatched emails will prevent the invitation from linking correctly.

### Choose a role

Select one of two roles:

- **Member** — Can manage assigned events, view sales, and assist with day-of operations. Cannot invite other members or remove admins.
- **Admin** — Full team access, including inviting and removing members, purchasing tokens, and managing subscription quota.

When in doubt, start with **Member** and promote to **Admin** later if needed.

### Click Invite member

Click the red **Invite member** button. ECTickets sends an invitation email to the address you entered.

</Steps>

<Callout type="warning">
  Invitations expire **7 days** after the email is sent. If an invite
  lapses, remove any pending entry and send a fresh invitation from
  Team settings.
</Callout>

## What the Invitee Receives

Once you send an invitation, the recipient gets an email notification from ECTickets (shown below in Traditional Chinese). The message includes the team name and an **Accept Invitation** button.

![Member invitation email](/assets/docs/organizer/invite-member/member-invitation-email-chinese.png)<br></br>
_Screenshot: Team invitation email with team name and accept button._

The invitee should:

1. **Log in** to their ECTickets account (or sign-up if they don't have one yet).
2. Click **Accept Invitation** in the email within **7 days**.
3. After accepting, they'll land in the shared Team Management Dashboard with the role you assigned.

## Accepting a Team Invitation

If an administrator from an existing Organizer Team invited you, use the link in your invitation email instead of the footer sign-up flow.

1. Open the invitation email and click **Accept Invitation**.
2. If you don't have an organizer account yet, sign-up using the same email address the invite was sent to.
3. After verification, you're added to the inviting team's dashboard with the role assigned by the admin.

> [!NOTE]
>
> If you already have an account, upgrade using the same email —
> your attendee history (e.g., past tickets) is preserved. Just enter your
> original password during setup.

<Callout type="info">
  Invitations expire **7 days** after the email is sent. If yours has
  expired, ask the team admin to send a new invitation from Team
  settings.
</Callout>

## Manage Existing Members

From the **Team members** list on the same page, you can:

- **Review roles** — confirm each person has the right level of access.
- **Remove a member** — click **Remove** next to their entry. Removed members lose access to the team dashboard immediately but keep their personal ECTickets account.

<Callout type="info">
  Keep at least one Admin on every team. If you're transferring
  ownership, invite the new admin first and confirm they've accepted
  before removing yourself.
</Callout>

## What Your Team Can Do Next

Once members join, everyone on the team shares access to the same event inventory and quota:

- **Purchase or request event tokens** — see [Creating Your First Event](/docs/organizer/getting-started/event-creation).
- **Create and configure events** — see [Event Management](/docs/organizer/event-management).
- **Run check-in on event day** — see [Check-In Guide](/docs/organizer/event-day/checkin).
- **Review sales and finalize payout** — see [Reports & Analytics](/docs/organizer/reports) and [Payout](/docs/organizer/post-event/payout).
